# Independent Review Checklist

## Contract

- [ ] The stated result matches `docs/specification.md`.
- [ ] Out-of-scope work is not implied as completed.
- [ ] The alert exception is described as intentional behavior.

## Evidence

- [ ] Every claim has a traceability entry.
- [ ] Historical results are labeled and limitations are adjacent.
- [ ] Synthetic screenshots are described as synthetic.
- [ ] Screenshot hashes match the manifest.

## Privacy

- [ ] Text scan is clean.
- [ ] Images contain no real records or private chrome.
- [ ] Video was watched end to end after export.
- [ ] Video works in a signed-out/incognito session.

## Agentic Practice

- [ ] Human and agent responsibilities are separated.
- [ ] Read-only diagnosis preceded mutation.
- [ ] Scope and stopping conditions are visible.
- [ ] Narrow and broad checks are recorded.
- [ ] Known blind spots are not hidden.

## Decision

Record exactly one result: `PASS`, `REVISE`, or `STOP`, with paths for every finding.
